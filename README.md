# Spectra by MZGuard

**Per-program network monitor and firewall for Windows (10 & 11, 64-bit).**

Spectra shows which programs on your Windows computer connect to the internet, where their data goes, and which country it reaches—all in one window. Understand bandwidth usage in real time or over the last 24 hours, and block untrusted programs, IP addresses, ranges, or domains in one click.

🌐 **Official Page & Download:** [mzguard.com/spectra.html](https://mzguard.com/spectra.html)

---

## Core Workflow: See, Understand, Decide

- **See:** Identify which program is connecting right now, to which host/IP, and in which country.
- **Understand:** Track how much data each application sends and receives over the last 5 minutes or the last 24 hours.
- **Decide:** Block a program, IP address, IP range, or domain. Rules are enforced directly through **Windows Firewall** and persist across restarts.

## Features

### Free Monitor (100% Local)
- **Live & 24h Traffic Graph:** Download/upload curves and traffic bursts marked per program.
- **Connection Table:** Columns sorted by application, host, traffic type, and country.
- **2D Map & 3D Globe:** Visualize live connection flows geographically.
- **LAN Scanner:** Discover and list devices on your local network.
- **Alerts & Resources View:** Real-time connection visibility without sending your traffic data off your PC.

### Pro Edition (One-Time Payment per Computer)
- **Application Firewall:** Inbound/outbound per-program blocking saved in Windows Firewall.
- **Compressed Log Archive:** Searchable daily archives of connections, alerts, and blocks with quick filters and **CSV / CEF** export.
- **AI Session Assistant:** Ask questions about active traffic and session summaries.

## Privacy & Requirements
- **OS:** Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit).
- **Permissions:** Requires **Administrator privileges** on startup to read byte counters and manage Windows Firewall rules (single instance).
- **Privacy:** The free monitor runs **100% locally**—your traffic, programs, and addresses stay on your machine. When using the optional Pro AI assistant, only your question and a session summary are forwarded without storing the conversation.
